01 ABOUT ME

Coaching should make fitness feel clearer, not more complicated.

I'm Krishna Kanth, a personal trainer with 2+ years of experience helping clients build strength, improve movement and develop healthier routines they can actually sustain.

I care about the details — how an exercise fits your body, whether your program is progressing, how your recovery is going, and whether your nutrition habits support the life you actually live.

My goal is simple: give you structure, teach you the “why” behind your training, and help you become confident enough to own your progress.

06 FAQ

Questions before you start?

Here are a few things people usually want to know before booking a consultation.

Do I need gym experience before working with you?+

No. Coaching can be adapted to your current experience level. The first step is understanding where you are now and building from there.

Do you help with nutrition too?+

Yes — I focus on practical nutrition habits that support training, energy and consistency. Medical nutrition therapy should be handled by a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.

What happens in the first consultation?+

We'll talk through your goals, training history, schedule, current challenges and what type of coaching support would make sense for you.

How do I get started?+

Use the consultation page to choose the next available time once your calendar link is connected, or submit your details so we can start the conversation.
